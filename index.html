About Robert Frank

An insider who kept asking questions.

Robert Frank writes speculative fiction at the intersection of technology and human vulnerability. His stories examine how our tools shape us — and what happens when the systems we depend on become the systems that trap us.

A lifelong tech enthusiast who grew up collecting gadgets instead of complaints, Frank brings an insider's eye to the promises technology makes and the costs it extracts. By day, he navigates the practical side of connected devices and digital infrastructure; by night, he explores the messy interpersonal fallout when innovation outpaces wisdom.

His work blends noir sensibility with the complicated intimacy of people trying to connect in an increasingly surveilled world.
